Your yearly numbers

Assumes you are not VAT-registered and that this is your only taxable income. Mixed-income earners get no ₱250,000 reduction under the 8% option.

Estimated yearly tax

Usual filing schedule
  • BIR Form 1701Q (quarterly income tax): May 15, Aug 15, Nov 15
  • BIR Form 1701 / 1701A (annual income tax): April 15 of the next year
  • BIR Form 2551Q (3% percentage tax): within 25 days after each quarter. Only for graduated-rate filers, not for the 8% option.

The 8% option is chosen at registration or on your first quarterly return and applies for the whole year. Deadlines that fall on weekends or holidays move to the next business day.

Selling websites: basics
  • Register with the BIR and get your Certificate of Registration
  • Issue BIR-registered invoices to clients
  • Keep books of accounts and records of your expenses
  • Watch the ₱3,000,000 gross limit: above it, the 8% option is not available and VAT applies

This tool is an estimate for planning, not tax advice and it does not file anything. Rates follow the TRAIN schedule as published for 2026. Confirm the details with the BIR (bir.gov.ph) or a tax professional.
